Lantern Book Club - fictional practice document

The Lantern Book Club meets every Wednesday at 6:30 PM in the Willow Room of the Maple Community Library. Meetings last 75 minutes. The club reads one book each month.

Membership is free. Members may borrow the current month's book from the library or bring their own copy. New members can attend without registering.

The club coordinator is Maya Chen. Members should send book suggestions to the coordinator at a meeting. Suggestions are collected during the first meeting of each month, and members vote at the second meeting.

The club keeps a small lending shelf. Each member may borrow at most two books from this shelf for 21 days. Members record their name and the return date in the paper lending notebook. There are no late fees, but members should return overdue books before borrowing more.

If the library closes because of bad weather, that week's meeting is cancelled. The next meeting follows the regular Wednesday schedule. There is no online replacement meeting.

The club has not selected a summer trip destination. This document does not list the library director's name or any club bank account details.
